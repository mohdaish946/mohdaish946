### 🚀 Featured Resume Projects

#### 1. 🛒 [Olist E-Commerce Data Analysis](https://github.com/mohdaish946/Olist_Ecommerce_Data_Analysis)
`Python` `PostgreSQL` `SQLAlchemy` `Power BI` `DAX`
- **Data Engineering & ETL:** Cleaned, validated, and merged **9 relational tables (99K+ orders, 1M+ rows)** in Python (`Pandas`) before loading into **PostgreSQL** via `SQLAlchemy`.
- **Advanced SQL Analytics:** Engineered complex queries using **CTEs and Window Functions (`LAG`, running totals)** to evaluate MoM revenue growth, customer repeat purchase rates, and the impact of late deliveries on review scores.
- **BI & Business Impact:** Built an interactive **Power BI** dashboard tracking **$15.84M Total Revenue**, **91.89% On-Time Delivery**, and top-performing categories to drive retention and operational decisions.

#### 2. 🧠 Customer Personality Analysis & Predictive Segmentation
`Python` `Scikit-learn` `XGBoost` `CatBoost` `FastAPI` `Docker`
- **Unsupervised Segmentation:** Processed customer behavioral metrics (spending, tenure, household structure) and applied **PCA with K-Means** to segment **2,240+ customers** into **3 distinct buyer personas**.
- **Supervised Classification:** Trained, benchmarked, and hyperparameter-tuned (**GridSearchCV**) multiple classifiers (**XGBoost, CatBoost, Random Forest**) to predict customer segments for new incoming users.
- **Production Deployment:** Deployed a low-latency **FastAPI** inference service and containerized the end-to-end ML pipeline using **Docker**.

#### 3. ✈️ Airport Traffic & Relational Database Analysis
`SQL Server` `T-SQL` `Database Normalization` `Data Quality & Reconciliation`
- **Schema Architecture:** Designed and normalized a **5-table SQL Server schema** (Primary/Foreign keys, `CHECK` constraints) from a **19,926-row** airline traffic dataset integrated with city population metrics.
- **Data Quality & Auditing:** Built source-to-target reconciliation and **18 automated `PASS/FAIL` data-quality checks**; detected and resolved a join fan-out bug that overstated passenger counts by **5%** and filtered false population matches via plausibility rules.
- **Analytical Reporting:** Developed reporting views, parameterized stored procedures with input validation, and **T-SQL** queries (CTEs, window functions, running totals), uncovering that **18 of 760 airports** handle 50% of all traffic and **Delta** operates **73%** of Atlanta's departures.
